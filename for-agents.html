FAQ

Trade partner FAQ

Do you work with retail travel agencies and outbound tour operators?

Yes. ROTW Travels is an established inbound DMC in Moscow. We provide confidential net tariffs, bespoke FIT planning, and group series for registered travel trade partners.

How quickly can we expect a customized B2B quotation?

Our Moscow operations desk typically prepares comprehensive FIT proposals within 24 hours. Complex multi-city or MICE programs are returned within 48 to 72 hours.

Do you provide visa invitation letters for agency clients?

Yes. As an authorized Russian tour operator, we issue official tourist invitation letters (vouchers and confirmation certificates) required for consular or e-visa applications.

What languages are available for guided excursions?

We provide certified, experienced local guides fluent in English, Russian, Arabic, and additional languages upon request.

Can you handle MICE, conferences, and incentive groups?

Yes. We manage end-to-end MICE logistics across Moscow, St Petersburg, Sochi, and Baikal, including conference halls, gala dinners, transport fleets, and special events.

How can an agency request your net rate sheet?

Fill out the net rate inquiry form below or email inbound@rotwtravels.com with your agency credentials and upcoming travel requirements.
