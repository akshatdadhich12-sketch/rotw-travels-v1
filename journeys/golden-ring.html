FAQ

Questions about this journey

When is the best time to take the Moscow and the Golden Ring journey?

May to September. We tailor the departure dates, pacing, and daily schedule around your preferred travel window.

What is included in this journey?

Itineraries are tailored per traveller. We provide invitation letters, private transfers, domestic trains or flights, hotels, and local guides. Detailed scope is confirmed in your written quote.

Do I need a visa to travel to Russia?

Visa requirements depend on your passport. Some travellers qualify for an electronic visa (e-visa) while others require a standard tourist visa with an official voucher and invitation letter. ROTW provides full visa support and invitation letters for your application.

Which languages do ROTW guides speak?

Our guides speak English, Russian, Arabic, and other major languages. Let us know your preferred language when planning your trip, and we will match the right guide for your journey.

What support is provided while we are travelling in Russia?

We arrange airport meet and assist upon arrival, private drivers, local guides, and direct emergency telephone support from our Moscow operations desk for the entire duration of your trip.

How do we book and confirm this journey?

Click 'Plan this journey' to send your preferred dates and group details. Our Moscow team will contact you with a customized draft proposal and confirmed pricing.
