    Frequently Asked Questions

    Planning Your Journey

    Everything you need to know about planning a custom trip to Russia with ROTW Travels.

    How do I plan a trip to Russia with ROTW?

    Start by choosing who is travelling, your travel dates, preferred destinations, and comfort style in our planner. We shape a tailored itinerary based on your choices. Every estimate is confirmed in writing by ROTW in a personal quote before anything is finalized.

    Does the planner give a final price?

    No, estimates shown in the planner are not binding offers. The estimated cost is always subject to confirmation by ROTW. We review seasonal availability, tailored hotels, and private guides before confirming the exact final price in writing in your formal itinerary quote.

    Is my information stored when I use the planner?

    No, your planning selections remain entirely inside your browser local storage on your device. We do not transmit or store your planner inputs on any remote web server. When you submit your plan, it opens a prefilled message directly in your local email app.

    Which budget bands does the planner use?

    The planner offers four distinct budget categories to reflect your preferred style of travel and comfort. These categories cover your land arrangements within Russia, excluding international flights. They guide our travel specialists in selecting appropriate boutique hotels, private transfers, and curated excursions.

    Can travel agents use the planner?

    Yes, travel agents can select the dedicated agent option within the trip planner. This routes your inquiry directly to our confidential B2B operations department in Moscow. You can also visit our For agents page to review our partnership terms and request wholesale net rates.
